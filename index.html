Map

Change the basemap or add trails with the layer button on the map.

Legend

Each water is coloured by the rarest species recorded in it. Grey = no fish records.

Waters and species come from the Government of Alberta FWMIS database: a species is listed if it has ever been recorded anywhere in that waterbody. National parks are not covered by FWMIS.

Seasons, limits and closures: always check the current Alberta Guide to Sportfishing Regulations, My Wild Alberta and, in national parks, Parks Canada fishing rules.
